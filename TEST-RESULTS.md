- 상태 200, 응답 10.3초
- 종류: answer
- 답변: Busan offers a vibrant street food scene with numerous spots to explore. Here are some popular areas and dishes to try:

BIFF Square and Gukje Market

Located in Nampo-dong, these adjacent markets are renowned for their street food offerings. BIFF Square is famous for ssiat hotteok, a sweet pancake filled with seeds and nuts, while Gukje Market offers a variety of traditional Korean snacks. (biffsquarebusan.com)

Jagalchi Fish Market

As Korea's largest seafood market, Jagalchi provides fresh seafood options, including fish cake skewers and grilled shellfish. The outdoor stalls along the waterfront are particularly popular for street food. (insidekoreanow.com)

Seomyeon Underground Shopping Street

This bustling commercial district features a lively underground food alley beneath the main shopping streets, offering a variety of street foods like tteokbokki, kimbap, and sundae combo sets. (insidekoreanow.com)

When visiting these areas, it's advisable to carry small bills, as many stalls are cash-only. Prices are generally affordable, allowing you to sample a variety of dishes without overspending. (insidekoreanow.com)

Enjoy your culinary adventure in Busan!

## 10. [transport] How do I get from Seoul Station to Busan, and which is better, KTX or bus?

- 답변: To travel from Seoul Station to Busan, you have two main options: the KTX high-speed train and the express bus. However, if budget is a primary concern and you're comfortable with a longer journey, the express bus is a viable alternative.
